Privacy policy

Last updated November 17, 2026. DarkDorms ("we") sells door strips to students at darkdorms.com.

What we collect

When you order: Stripe collects your name, email, phone, dorm, room and card details. We never see or store your full card number.

Restock alerts: the first name, phone and dorm you put in the email you send us.

Analytics: if you accept cookies, anonymous page views and button clicks, with no name or contact details.

How we use it

To deliver your order, text you about drop-off, answer your questions, and see which parts of the site work. We don't sell your information.

Who we share it with

Stripe (payments), our analytics provider, and our email provider, only as needed to run the store.

Your choices

Email me@darkdorms.com to see or delete your information, or reply STOP to any text.
