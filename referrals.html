How payouts work

  1. Rep shares their QR or link. A visitor who opens it is tagged for 30 days on that phone. The last rep link they opened wins.
  2. Visitor buys on Stripe. Their checkout carries the rep's code, so this page can count it.
  3. Sales wait out the refund window (7 days) as "pending", then move to "owed". Refunded orders earn nothing.
  4. You pay the rep by Venmo or Cash App, then add the payment to api/_lib/referrers.mjs so "owed" goes back to $0.
